Project Overview

This project investigates marine gross primary production (GPP) and community respiration (CR) using light-dark bottle observations. The long-term goal is to integrate environmental variables, including sea surface temperature (SST) and chlorophyll-a, to improve predictive modeling.

Current Dataset

Original observations: 3,188

Observations after preliminary duplicate removal: 3,173

Primary outcomes: GPP and CR

Baseline Model Results

Random Forest regression models are used to predict log-transformed GPP and CR from spatial, temporal, and depth-related features.

Updated model comparisons will be added after validating the cleaned dataset.
