Project Overview

This project investigates marine gross primary production (GPP) and community respiration (CR) using light-dark bottle observations.

The long-term goal is to integrate environmental variables, including sea surface temperature (SST) and chlorophyll-a, to improve predictive modeling and better understand marine productivity patterns.

Current Dataset

Original observations: 3,188

Observations after preliminary duplicate removal: 3,173

Primary outcomes: Gross Primary Production (GPP) and Community Respiration (CR)

Baseline Model Results

Random Forest regression models are used to predict log-transformed GPP and CR using latitude, longitude, depth, year, and month.

The initial models use an 80/20 train-test split. Updated results will be reported after validating the cleaned dataset.
